# E-Commerce Sales Analysis: Business Insights

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes a Superstore-style retail dataset containing 9,977 records after removing duplicate rows. The analysis focuses on sales, profit, product categories, regional performance, customer segments, and discounts.

## 2. Key Business Insights

### Insight 1: Technology Leads in Sales

The Technology category generated total sales of $836,154.10, the highest among the three product categories.

**Business implication:** Technology products are an important contributor to overall sales. Their profitability should also be monitored before making decisions about inventory or promotions.

### Insight 2: The West Region Has the Highest Profit

The West region generated a total profit of $108,330.15, outperforming the other regions in the dataset.

**Business implication:** The business can examine the West region's product mix and sales practices to identify approaches that may work in other regions.

### Insight 3: Discounts Have a Negative Relationship with Profit

The correlation between Discount and Profit is -0.22, indicating a weak negative linear relationship in this dataset.

**Business implication:** Discount policies should be reviewed alongside product costs and profit margins. Correlation alone does not establish causation.

### Insight 4: Sales and Profit Have a Positive Relationship

The correlation between Sales and Profit is 0.48, indicating a moderate positive linear relationship.

**Business implication:** Higher sales values tend to be associated with higher profit values, but individual transactions and product categories may still generate losses.

### Insight 5: Profitability Varies Across Regions

The West region generated $108,330.15 in profit, followed by the East at $91,506.37, the South at $46,749.71, and Central at $39,655.97.

**Business implication:** Regional performance should be reviewed separately to identify opportunities to improve profitability in lower-performing regions.

## 3. Business Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Review Discount Policies

Analyze profit margins across discount levels, product categories, and subcategories. Set discount limits based on profitability rather than sales volume alone.

### Recommendation 2: Investigate Regional Performance

Compare product mix, sales, discounts, and profit across regions. Use findings from the higher-profit regions to identify practical improvement opportunities elsewhere.

## 4. Conclusion

The analysis shows that Technology leads in sales and the West region leads in profit. The negative relationship between discounts and profit suggests that discounting deserves further investigation. An interactive Power BI dashboard can help stakeholders explore these patterns by category, region, and customer segment.
